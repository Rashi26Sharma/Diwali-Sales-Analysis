# 🪔 Diwali Sales Analysis: End-to-End Data Analytics Project

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project analyzing Diwali sales data using **Python** and **Power BI** to uncover customer purchasing behavior, revenue drivers, customer segments, and actionable business recommendations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze festive season sales data and answer key business questions such as:

- Who are the highest-value customers?
- Which states and regions generate maximum revenue?
- Which product categories perform the best?
- What customer segments should marketing target?
- How can businesses increase festive season sales?

The project combines data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, statistical testing, customer segmentation, and interactive dashboards to generate business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail companies spend heavily during the Diwali season but often lack a clear understanding of:

- Customer demographics
- Regional buying behavior
- Product performance
- High-value customer segments

This project converts raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision making.

---

## 🔍 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning

- Removed unnecessary columns
- Handled missing values
- Corrected data types
- Standardized column names
- Created new business features

---

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis

Analyzed purchasing behavior across:

- Gender
- Age Groups
- Marital Status
- Occupation
- States
- Zones
- Product Categories

Generated more than **20 visualizations** for business understanding.

---

### 3. Statistical Analysis

Performed:

- Welch's T-Test
- One-Way ANOVA
- Outlier Detection using IQR

These tests validated observed purchasing patterns.

---

### 4. Customer Segmentation

Implemented **K-Means Clustering** using:

- Purchase Frequency
- Monetary Value
- Average Order Value

Identified four customer segments:

- ⭐ VIP Champions
- ⭐ Loyal High-Spenders
- ⭐ Regular Customers
- ⭐ Bargain Customers

## 📈 Key Insights

- Female customers generated the majority of revenue.
- Customers aged **26–35** contributed the highest sales.
- Married customers showed stronger purchasing behavior.
- Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Delhi, and Madhya Pradesh were the top-performing states.
- Electronics recorded the highest average order value.
- Food and Clothing were the highest-selling product categories.
- Customer segmentation identified high-value VIP customers for personalized marketing campaigns.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing campaigns on high-value customer personas.
- Increase inventory for high-demand product categories.
- Launch personalized offers for VIP customers.
- Expand marketing efforts in underperforming regions.
- Use bundle offers and cross-selling strategies to improve average order value.
